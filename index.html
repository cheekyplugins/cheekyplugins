Why I made these

Plenty of plugins came close. Few did quite the right thing.

I've been making records for 20 years, including with my band Televisor, whose releases on Monstercat have racked up millions of plays on Spotify and YouTube.

In that time I've bought more plugins than I'd like to admit. Lots of them nearly did it for me. They sounded great but buried the one control I needed, or did nine things when I wanted one, or brought my laptop to its knees the moment I put them on a few tracks.

AI coding tools are what made it possible for one musician to build his own. But these aren't plugins I typed a wish into a chatbot for and shipped. Each one started as a gap in my own sessions. I decided what every plugin should do and who it's for, which features the big names leave out, what every control is called and how it should look and behave. Then I tested them on my own tracks until they earned their place.

They're also built to be light. My own machine is an 8 GB MacBook Air. If a plugin makes it sweat, it doesn't ship.

Tom (Televisor), Cheeky Audio

Installing

There's one extra step, because Apple wants a paid certificate before it will wave an installer through without asking. Nothing is wrong with the plugins.

  1. DownloadGrab a single plugin or the whole bundle. Each one is a DMG with an installer and a Read Me.
  2. Run the installerDouble-click the .pkg. macOS will say it can't verify the developer. Click Done.
  3. Open AnywaySystem Settings › Privacy & Security. Scroll down, click Open Anyway, enter your password.
  4. RescanRestart your DAW or rescan plugins. Look under Cheeky Audio.

Questions

Are they really free?

Yes, for the whole beta. In return, tell me what breaks and which presets sound like a kazoo.

Windows?

Not yet. The Mac versions come first. Windows is on the list once they've settled.

Is it safe to click Open Anyway?

The warning appears for any installer that isn't signed with Apple's paid developer certificate. The installer only puts the AU and VST3 files into your Plug-Ins folders.

How do I uninstall?

Delete the plugin from /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components and /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3. In Finder, use Go › Go to Folder.

Found a bug?

Lovely, in a manner of speaking. Report it on GitHub with your DAW, your macOS version and what you were doing when it went wrong.

Tell me when it's out

One email when a new plugin lands or a beta becomes 1.0. No spam, no "just checking in".
