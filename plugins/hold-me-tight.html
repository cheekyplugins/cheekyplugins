Why you'll like it

See the squeeze

A scrolling display of input, output and gain reduction, a draggable threshold line, a transfer curve with a live dot, and momentary loudness in LUFS.

Eight styles

Clean, Vintage, Opto, Vocal, Glue, Master, Smack and Pump: eight ways of reacting to your audio, from invisible to unashamedly pumping.

The deep stuff

External sidechain with filters and an EQ bell, up to 20 ms lookahead, mid/side modes, up to 4x oversampling and true parallel dry gain, all under the duvet.

What it sounds like

Each style borrows the behaviour of a classic type of compressor.

Opto has the slow, soft glow of a 60s optical leveller, with that two-stage release that makes vocals sit without anyone noticing. Vintage uses a feedback design like the grabby valve and FET boxes of the 60s and 70s. Glue is the console bus-compressor trick for making a mix sound like one band. Pump breathes on purpose, French-house style. Smack lets the first few milliseconds of every hit through.

Clean and Master stay out of the way: transparent, with Master still catching fast peaks.
