Little Synths

Tell me sweet little synths.

A 10-voice polysynth for that glossy late-80s soft-rock shimmer: lush analogue pads, glassy FM keys and chiming plucked strings, with a More Drama button for when it needs to fill a stadium.

Why you'll like it

Six waves, two oscillators

Sine, square, saw and triangle, plus Chime, a modelled plucked string, and FM, which runs from electric piano to bells on one knob.

Mix means character

One knob per oscillator does the musical thing for that wave: pulse width, stacked-saw thickness, warmth, skew, brightness or EP-to-bells.

More Drama

Pushes chorus, width and a big reverb together. It's the button you'll press first and then pretend you didn't need.

What it sounds like

Think Tango in the Night-era shimmer: wide, chorused and slightly too beautiful.

The Chime wave doubles itself with a late, slightly detuned second take, like the slowed-down tape guitars on those records. The FM side gives you the glassy keys every 80s ballad was legally required to have.

Under it all is tape wobble, ping-pong echo and an eight-line reverb, set by the presets and automatable from your DAW.
