Squeeze Me!

A proper compressor for people who don't know what release does.

Tell it what you're compressing, turn one knob, and it does what a good engineer would. When you're curious, open the bonnet and see the real settings moving.

Why you'll like it

Pick the source

Vocals, Drums, Bass, Guitar, Keys or Mix Bus. Each loads an engineer's starting point for attack, release, knee, sidechain filter and ratio.

One knob that means it

The threshold follows your audio, so 50% does roughly the same job on a whisper or a full mix. It listens when audio starts, then tracks slowly so quiet verses stay quieter than choruses.

It tells you the truth

A plain-English readout says what it's doing and whether that's sensible for the source. Auto Level matches loudness in and out, so louder can't fool you into thinking it's better.

What it sounds like

Three characters, three classic flavours of squeeze.

Smooth averages the level and lets the release slow down as it works harder, the gentle levelling you'd want on a vocal. Punchy holds back the attack so drums and bass keep their snap. Aggressive grabs fast with a hard knee for that in-your-face, flattened sound.

Blend mixes in the uncompressed signal for parallel compression: the body of a squashed sound with the life of the original.
